7. Recommendation

Don't deploy v9.3 BTC standalone. Keep observing.
  • v9.3 BTC standalone is not a 90% WR strategy at any reachable threshold on the current emission ceiling. Best is 71.4% WR at thr 0.73 (n=49).
  • v9.3 has no additive lift over v9.2 in AND-filter ensembles — the v9.3+v9.2 best cell merely matches the v9.2-only contribution, and v9.3+v12 underperforms v9.2+v12.
  • The v9.2 + v12 AND filter (0.75/0.55) remains the leader at 92.86% WR (n=28, LB 77.4%) on this 221-window dataset. Promote that, not v9.3.
  • Wait for more data above 0.78: at current emission rates (~3 windows/day > 0.79), an additional ~8–10 days of GHOST data is needed to give the high-threshold tail enough sample to evaluate whether a true 90% pocket exists.
  • Investigate why the ceiling stalled. Only 1 window above 0.86 in 24h suggests PR #582 didn't fully unblock the feature path — check whether the 20:56 expansion was a one-shot.

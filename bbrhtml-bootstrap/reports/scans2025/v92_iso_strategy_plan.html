1 · Headline

Result
Isotonic calibration of probability_lgb_v9_2 drops test ECE from 0.1644 to 0.0216 (-87%) and Brier -10.6% — both far exceeding the canonical promotion gate (training/iso_refit_v9_1_v12.py: ECE ≥ 30%, Brier ≥ 5%). At the live UP gate (p92 ≥ 0.72), applying iso gives +44% qualifying ticks at +1.6pp WR — tick-level, on a fully held-out 2-day test split.
Important caveat
Numbers above are tick-level qualifying events, not first-fire trade-level events. Direction of effect is robust. Magnitude in daily-trade terms needs a first-fire simulator (open TODO).

Why this matters
Per-day WR varies wildly (73.5% / 98.8% / 81.7%) — regime drift is real and iso does NOT immunize against it. Iso fixes aggregate calibration; it doesn't predict which day you're in. Promotion plan must include monitoring triggers for per-day WR drift.

3 · The strategic fork

Two ways to ship the iso candidate, each with different risk/reward and different blast radius if something goes wrong.

Option A: modify the existing v9_2_raw_lgb strategy REJECT

Apply iso transform to probability_lgb_v9_2 at the engine load path, keep thresholds at 0.72/0.20. Behaviour of the live strategy changes overnight.

  • +44% volume immediately, +1.6pp WR projected (tick-level)
  • One change, one config edit, no new strategy plumbing
  • BUT: blast radius is the entire live strategy's PnL — no shadow period, no isolated test
  • If iso behaves differently in production (regime shift, residual under/over-confidence at 0.65-0.80), you find out via real PnL hit
  • Per feedback_no_auto_promote.md, every promotion is manual — but this is still a higher-stakes promotion than a new strat

Option B: ship a NEW strategy variant RECOMMENDED

Launch v9_2_raw_lgb_iso alongside the existing strat. Same gate logic but probability is iso-calibrated. Low max_collateral_pct for first weeks. Run in parallel for direct A/B.

  • Existing strategy continues to trade with its proven 0.72/0.20 gates — zero perturbation
  • New strategy quantifies the actual lift (or absence of lift) under real conditions
  • Low max_bid (e.g. $5 vs current $20) caps PnL exposure during shadow period
  • If new strat underperforms in live, kill it; existing strat unaffected
  • If it overperforms, gradually raise its budget while ramping down the old one
  • Aligns with Billy's vote per session conversation
Recommendation
Option B — new strategy variants at low max bid. Lower blast radius, cleaner attribution, fits the manual-promote discipline.

4 · Proposed approach — NEW strategy variants

Three new strategies, all gated at the same statistical regime as v9_2_raw_lgb but trading on the calibrated probability. Each isolates a different verification question.

📐 v9_2_iso_volmatch — sanity-check baseline

Purpose: verify iso doesn't change real behaviour vs raw at matched volume.

Gate: p92_cal ≥ 0.797 UP, p92_cal ≤ 0.098 DOWN. These calibrated thresholds correspond to raw 0.72 and raw 0.20 by construction.

Expected: ~same fires/day as existing strat, ~same WR. If different: iso has a bug, halt rollout.

Budget: $5 max_position (vs $20 live)

🚀 v9_2_iso_expand — the headline thesis

Purpose: harvest the +44% volume that iso surfaces at the same numerical threshold.

Gate: p92_cal ≥ 0.72 UP, p92_cal ≤ 0.20 DOWN.

Expected: ~1.4× fires/day vs existing, ~1pp higher WR. If different: tick→trade conversion is non-trivial or regime drift dominates.

Budget: $5 max_position, then ramp on positive shadow data

🛡️ v9_2_iso_strict — higher-conviction variant

Purpose: the "tightening" lever from #531 that DID work at scale. Half the volume of headline variant but +5pp WR projected.

Gate: p92_cal ≥ 0.85 UP, p92_cal ≤ 0.12 DOWN.

Expected: ~0.5× fires/day vs existing, ~95% WR. Useful if the headline variant suffers from regime drift.

Budget: $5 max_position. Could be the survival variant if expand underperforms.

🚫 NOT proposing

  • Tiered band-dependent thresholds — falsified on full window (#531 correction)
  • Extension-zone strategy (eval_offset > 210) — cliff confirmed at scale
  • Joint p92 ∩ p91 strategy — only 4 days of v9.1 data (#531 appendix G)
  • Iso applied directly to the live v9_2_raw_lgb — Option A above

STAGE 6 — D+9
Decision gate

If any of the three variants show realized WR within 1pp of projection AND volume within 25% of projection: promote that variant to LIVE at $5 max_position. If divergence > 25%, extend shadow period or re-run analysis.

STAGE 7 — D+9 to D+30
Live at $5 cap

Run promoted variant in parallel with existing v9_2_raw_lgb. Daily WR audit. Per-hour-of-day audit. Wallet truth reconciliation.

STAGE 8 — D+30+
Ramp or kill

Promote to full max_position OR cut. Then consider retiring v9_2_raw_lgb (the raw-uncalibrated version) if the iso variant clearly dominates over 4 weeks of live data.

7 · Decision points (where Billy presses go / no-go)

  1. Stage 1 outcome: do first-fire trade-level numbers confirm the +44% tick-level finding? If trade-level lift is < 20%, scope down to volmatch-only.
  2. Stage 2 outcome: is there an identity iso already loaded? If yes, this is a refit (lower bar); if no, this is a first-fit (higher bar, more diligence).
  3. Stage 6 decision: which variant(s) to promote? If multiple meet bar, promote the conservative (volmatch) first.
  4. Stage 8 decision: retire raw v9_2_raw_lgb? Only if iso variant has 4+ weeks at higher PnL/trade with no negative tails.

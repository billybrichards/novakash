Why dir_acc looks low (~54%) — and why that's fine

The LoRA's dir_acc measures the foundation forecast direction — predicting whether the next bar closes above or below the current one. 54% on raw foundation is normal; the LGB stack on top (v9, v9.1, v10, v12) is what extracts tradeable edge. The LoRA's job is to sharpen the quantile distribution shape, not to predict direction directly.

Why this is the highest-value model on the box for ETH/XRP

The 15m LoRA was fine-tuned with 1,253 windows from each of BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP (5,012 total) — making the resulting embeddings + classifier head the only model in the fleet that genuinely transfers cross-asset. The 5m LoRA, v9, v9.1, v10, and v12 are all BTC-only. So when you ask "what model handles ETH 15m?" — this is it. Top-20% confidence filter (p ≥ 0.62 or ≤ 0.38) gives 80.8% accuracy; tighten to top-10% for 88.2%. v_eth_15m_classifier_top20 and top10 in PR #474 deploy exactly this.

Lift is statistically significant on every horizon (Wilson 95% CI doesn't cross zero).
